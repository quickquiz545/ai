Why Move from Google Workspace to Microsoft 365?

  • Advanced governance and eDiscovery aligned to Microsoft Purview.
  • Deeper Windows, Intune, and Azure AD integration for device and identity control.
  • Teams-first collaboration tied to SharePoint and OneDrive for unified content.
  • Licensing consolidation and security tooling alignment across the Microsoft cloud.

Scope of Migration – What We Move

Gmail → Exchange Online

Mailbox, labels to folders, delegates, and rules mapped.

Google Drive / Shared Drives → OneDrive & SharePoint

Permissions, shared links, and ownership re-homed.

Google Calendar → Outlook Calendar

Recurring meetings, resources, and timezone parity.

Google Contacts → Outlook Contacts

Contacts, labels, and GAL alignment.

Google Chat, Spaces → Teams

Channels, chat history, and files aligned to Teams structure.

Google Groups → M365 Groups / Distribution Lists

Membership, moderation, and posting policies recreated.
