What We Move

Exchange → Gmail

Mailboxes, aliases, delegates, rules, and retention labels mapped.

OneDrive → Google Drive

Permissions, sharing links, and version history where supported.

SharePoint → Drive folders / Shared Drives

Sites, libraries, metadata, and sharing mapped into Google Drive structures.

Teams → Google Chat / Meet

Channels, chat, files, and meeting policies translated to Google Chat and Meet.

Why Organizations Switch
